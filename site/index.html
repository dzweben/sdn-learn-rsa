Social-feedback learning: RSA and inter-subject correlation

Thirty-three adolescents completed four runs of a social-evaluation learning task during fMRI. Single-subject GLMs estimated run-wise feedback responses for four peers, without spatial smoothing. Three analyses relate those responses to trait social anxiety.

LEARN study · AFNI no-blur pipeline · 33 subjects · 4 runs

The three findings

#AnalysisResultFDR
1Model Alignment RSA Higher social anxiety tracks rostral-ACC peer representations that sharpen across runs (SA by run, b = +0.032) q = .025
2Temporal ISC Higher social anxiety tracks reduced synchrony with the group: rACC rho = -0.53, aMCC rho = -0.50 q = .052 / .059
3Whole-brain ISC (Schaefer-400) One parcel survives across the cortex, dorsal medial frontal (RH_Cont_Cing), rho = -0.65 q = .017
